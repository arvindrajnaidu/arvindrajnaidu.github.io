How it works

Open a news site from your list (or type one in), then tap Filter. You'll see one sample of every kind of text on the page. Tap the ones you want to read, then tap Read. News Lens remembers your choice for every page on that site.

Change a filter

While reading, tap the sliders icon at the top to pick again. If the site has a suggested filter, Reset to suggested brings it back.

See the original page

Tap the globe icon to slide the original page back.

Remove a site

On the home screen, swipe a site to the left and tap the trash icon. Its filter is forgotten too.
