News Lens

Privacy policy

News Lens collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The sites on your home screen and the filters you choose are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

Once a day at most, the app downloads a public list of suggested filters from GitHub (gist.githubusercontent.com). The request carries no information about you or what you read. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that request.

News sites

News Lens shows news sites in a built-in web view, the same way a browser does. Those sites may use cookies and their own tracking under their own privacy policies; News Lens doesn't share anything with them beyond what any browser visit does.
